# IAM — I Am Many

> Status: **exploratory** (track but don't push)
> Created: 2026-05-13

## What this is

Exploration of what it looks like for a single human (CEO) to operate a company where the rest of the workforce is autonomous AI agents.

The name plays on:
- **IAM** — Identity and Access Management, the technical substrate of any agent workforce (who acts for whom, with what permissions)
- *"I am Legion, for we are many"* — the CEO speaks, but the voice is plural
- A title-flip on *They Are Billions* — in the game *they* are the threat; here *they* are the workforce

## What this is not (yet)

- Not committed to a deliverable (personal OS / open-source framework / SaaS / pure research — all open)
- Not on a timeline
- Not competing with priority projects (Passemarche, Bangun)

## Open questions

- What is this *for me*? Personal operating system for my own portfolio, or generic framework, or a product?
- What's the actual unit of delegation — a task? a role? a domain (CFO-agent, COO-agent)?
- What's the right substrate — existing tool (CrewAI / MetaGPT / AutoGen / LangGraph), or custom?
- Where does "CEO judgement" stay, and where does it get encoded?
